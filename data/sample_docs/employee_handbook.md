# Northwind Robotics Employee Handbook

## Working hours and remote work

Northwind Robotics operates a hybrid model. Employees are expected on site in the Cairo office on Tuesdays and Thursdays; the remaining days may be worked remotely. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Cairo time, during which everyone should be reachable on Slack. Outside of core hours, teams agree their own schedules.

Fully remote arrangements require written approval from both the line manager and the People team, and are reviewed every six months.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to Egyptian public holidays. Leave accrues at two days per month. Up to five unused days may be carried over into the first quarter of the following year; any remaining balance is forfeited on 31 March.

Leave requests must be submitted in the Workday portal at least ten working days in advance for absences longer than three days. Managers should respond within three working days.

## Sick leave

Employees may take up to ten days of paid sick leave per year without a medical certificate. Absences longer than three consecutive days require a doctor's note uploaded to Workday within five days of returning to work.

## Parental leave

Birthing parents are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks of fully paid leave, which must be taken within the first year after the birth or adoption.

## Learning budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD, which can be spent on courses, conferences, books or certifications relevant to their role. Requests above 500 USD require manager approval in advance. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Equipment

New joiners receive a laptop (MacBook Pro 14-inch or ThinkPad X1 Carbon), a monitor and a headset. Equipment is refreshed every three years. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed through Expensify. Receipts are required for any item above 15 USD. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly payroll.

## Code of conduct

Northwind Robotics is committed to a respectful, inclusive workplace. Harassment, discrimination or retaliation of any kind is not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with a manager, the People team, or anonymously through the EthicsPoint hotline.
